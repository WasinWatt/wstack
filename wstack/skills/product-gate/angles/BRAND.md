# BRAND

Question: does the new copy sound and look like the rest of the product?

- Read the repo's design or brand guide (`DESIGN.md` or the file the profile names) and two neighbouring pages before judging.
- Voice: the product's name for itself, its pronouns and politeness particles, sentence case, and the words it uses for features, as the facts title them. A feature named differently from its fact title → `fix`.
- Visual: colours, type and components come from the design tokens and component library the guide names, not new one-offs.
- No promise of a roadmap, a date or an outcome the facts do not state.
