# WHY

Question: is there a real problem, for real people, with evidence?

- The Why names a problem, not a solution restated ("users want a 50-file cap" restates; "accountants download a month of receipts, 60–120 files, in 4 rounds" is a problem).
- Each piece of evidence is checkable: a linked issue or customer ask, a data query and its result, a support thread. A claim about how many or how often with no source → `question` to its owner.
- The Goal follows from the Why. A Goal that would not fix the stated problem → `fix`.
- An internal card names who on the team is slowed down and by how much.
